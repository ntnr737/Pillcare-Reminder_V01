## Full Description (4000 chars max)

PillCare Reminder helps you and your family build consistent medication routines. Whether you manage one tablet a day or a complex multi-medication schedule, PillCare keeps you on track.

**Smart medication reminders**
Set customised reminder times for each medication. Reminders fire even on silent mode. Never miss a dose again.

**Dose tracking with instant offline support**
Mark doses as taken, skipped, or missed with a single tap. Works completely offline — your data is saved locally and syncs automatically when you reconnect.

**Caregiver alerts via WhatsApp**
Connect a family member or trusted person. They receive a WhatsApp message every evening with your daily adherence summary, so they can support you without intruding on your day.

**Nearby pharmacies and hospitals**
Find the nearest medical stores, diagnostic labs, and hospitals using your current location. Get walking or driving directions without leaving the app.

**Progress and adherence tracking**
View your 30-day adherence chart, current streak, and daily dose history. See exactly which days you stayed on track and which you missed.

**AI wellness insights**
Receive personalised wellness tips based on your medications and adherence patterns, powered by AI.

**Yoga therapy recommendations**
Get yoga and breathing exercise recommendations tailored to your medications, sourced from Ministry of AYUSH guidelines. Set a daily yoga reminder.

**Brand to generic lookup**
Find the generic equivalent of any branded medicine and understand your options.

**Doctor appointments**
Store upcoming doctor visits with date, time, location, and phone number. Get reminders before each appointment. Call your doctor directly from the app.

**Complete data export**
Download your full health history — doses, measurements, activities, and mood — as a CSV file. Your data always belongs to you.

**Privacy and security**
Your health data is encrypted and never sold. We comply with India's DPDP Act 2023 and GDPR. You can delete your account and all data at any time.

PillCare Reminder is designed for patients, caregivers, and anyone who wants to build better health habits. It is a reminder tool only and does not provide medical advice. Always follow your doctor's instructions.
