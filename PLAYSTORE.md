⚕️ PILLCARE REMINDER — YOUR AI HEALTH COMPANION

PillCare Reminder is a smart medication management app designed to help you and your family stay on top of your health routine. Whether you're managing a complex medication schedule or caring for a loved one, PillCare makes it simple and reliable.

⚠️ MEDICAL DISCLAIMER: This app is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or health condition. It is a reminder and tracking tool only. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional before making changes to your medication or treatment plan.

🔔 SMART REMINDERS
• Timely medication reminders with sound and vibration
• Morning, afternoon, evening and night dose schedules
• Never miss a dose with persistent alerts
• Mark doses as Taken or Missed with one tap

📊 HEALTH TRACKING
• Weekly adherence percentage with colour-coded bar charts
• 30-day dose history with full log
• Track blood pressure, glucose, weight, SpO2 and more
• Log activities: steps, exercise, yoga, sleep

👨‍👩‍👧 FAMILY CARE

• Doctor appointment scheduling with reminders

🧘 AI-POWERED WELLNESS
• Daily AI health insight based on your medications
• Personalised yoga routines matched to your medication schedule
• Brand to generic drug name resolver (powered by OpenFDA)
• Prescription photo scanner — verify with your pharmacist

💊 MEDICATION MANAGEMENT
• Add medications with dosage, form, schedule and stock tracking
• Automatic refill reminders when stock runs low
• Generic name auto-resolution for cost savings
• Brand name to generic: just type the brand, we find the generic

🔒 PRIVACY FIRST
• Your health data is encrypted end-to-end
• We never sell your data to advertisers
• Export all your data at any time as CSV
• Delete your account and all data at any time

📱 OFFLINE FIRST

## Data Safety Form Answers

### Data collected and shared

| Data type | Collected | Shared | Required | Encrypted in transit | Encrypted at rest |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Name | Yes | No | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Email address | Yes | No | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| User IDs (Google sub) | Yes | No | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Health info (medications) | Yes | No — except AI processing | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Health info (measurements) | Yes | No — except AI processing | No | Yes | Yes |
| App activity | Yes | No | No | Yes | Yes |

### Purposes for data collection
- App functionality (reminders, tracking)
- Account management (sign-in, profile)
- Analytics (adherence, streak)

### Data shared with third parties
- **Groq AI**: Medication names + adherence data sent for AI insight generation. No PII included.
- **OpenFDA**: Medication brand names only (no personal data) for drug lookup.
- **WasenderAPI**: Caregiver phone number + summary message for WhatsApp alerts.
- **Google**: Authentication only via Google Sign-In.

### Does your app allow users to delete their data?
Yes — via Support → Delete Account, or by emailing support@pillcare.app
