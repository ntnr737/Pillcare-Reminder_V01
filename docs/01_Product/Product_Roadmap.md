# PillCare - Product Roadmap (2026 - 2027)

## 1. Vision Statement
To build India's most trusted offline-first medication adherence and preventive health intelligence platform, bridging the gap between patients, family caregivers, and clinical care teams.

## 3. Feature Breakdown & Timeline

### Q3 2026 (Completed)
- [x] High-precision local notifications & exact alarm scheduling.
- [x] Caregiver Alert escalation via WhatsApp template engine.
- [x] Admin Control Center (`admin.pillcare.in`) with 2-way manual overrides.
- [x] Refill Radar inventory automation.

### Q4 2026 (Active)
- [x] Healthcare Intelligence Radar (Adherence, Refill GMV, Risk Heatmap).
- [x] JP Morgan Financial KPIs (MRR, ARR, ARPU, LTV, Churn).
- [ ] iOS HealthKit Integration & WatchOS Companion App.
- [ ] Direct WhatsApp Bot for 1-click dose confirmation.

### Q1 2027 (Future Exploration)
- [ ] Hospital OPD Patient Portal integration.
- [ ] BLE Smart Pill Bottle hardware synchronization.
- [ ] Pharmacovigilance adverse event reporting API.
