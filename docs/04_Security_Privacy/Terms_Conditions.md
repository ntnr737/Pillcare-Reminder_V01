## 1. Medical Disclaimer (Non-Diagnostic Advisory Service)
PillCare is an assistive medication reminder and health tracking utility. **PillCare is NOT a licensed medical provider and does NOT provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment recommendations.** Always consult a qualified physician or pharmacist before starting, stopping, or altering any medication schedule.

---

## 2. Emergency Situations
PillCare is NOT an emergency response system. In case of a medical emergency or severe drug overdose, immediately contact your local emergency services (e.g. 112 / 108) or visit the nearest hospital emergency room.
