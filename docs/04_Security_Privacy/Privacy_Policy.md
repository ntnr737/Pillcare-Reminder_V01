# PillCare - Privacy Policy & Data Sovereignty

**Last Updated**: 15 September 2026

## 1. Our Commitment to Patient Privacy
PillCare is committed to protecting patient health information. We adhere strictly to HIPAA (Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act), DISHA (Digital Information Security in Healthcare Act), and GDPR guidelines.

---

## 2. Core Privacy Guarantees
1. **Zero Sale of Health Data**: We NEVER sell, rent, monetise, or commercialize patient health metrics or medication schedules to third parties or advertisers.
2. **Local Storage Sovereignty**: Medication schedules and logs remain encrypted on your device via SQLite (`AsyncStorage`).
3. **Caregiver Escalation Controls**: Caregiver notifications are sent ONLY to contacts explicitly authorized by the user.

---

## 3. Data Collected & Purpose
- **Medication Names & Timings**: For firing local alarms and calculating adherence.
- **Caregiver Contact Information**: For emergency WhatsApp/SMS missed-dose escalation.
- **Aggregated Analytics**: Anonymous, de-identified adherence statistics for system performance tuning.

---

## 4. User Rights (GDPR & DISHA)
Users have the absolute right to:
- Export their complete health logs in CSV/PDF format.
- Instantly purge their entire history from local device and cloud servers ("Right to be Forgotten").
